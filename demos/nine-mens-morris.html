How to Play

Nine Men's Morris · Three Phases

Phase 1 — Placing
  1. Each player has 9 pieces. Take turns placing one piece per turn onto any empty point on the board.
  2. If you form a mill (three of your pieces in a row along any line), you may immediately remove one of your opponent's pieces from the board. You cannot remove a piece that is part of a mill unless no other pieces are available.
Phase 2 — Moving
  1. Once all pieces are placed, players take turns sliding one piece to an adjacent empty point along a line.
  2. Forming a mill again allows you to remove one of the opponent's pieces.
  3. A player loses when reduced to 2 pieces, or when they have no legal moves.
Phase 3 — Flying
  1. When a player is reduced to exactly 3 pieces, they may fly — moving any piece to any empty point on the board, not just adjacent ones.
