How to Play

The Royal Game of Ur · ~2600 BC

  1. Each player has 7 pieces starting off the board. Roll 4 dice — each die scores 0 or 1. Your roll total (0–4) is how many squares to move.
  2. Enter a piece onto your first square by using a roll. Move any one piece forward by the roll amount each turn.
  3. Both players share the middle row (squares 5–12 of the path). You cannot land on your own pieces.
  4. Capture: Landing on an opponent's piece in the shared section sends it back off the board.
  5. Rosette squares (✿) are safe — pieces here cannot be captured. Landing on a rosette also grants a free extra roll!
  6. To bear a piece off, you must roll the exact number needed to exit. A roll of 0 forfeits your turn.
  7. First player to bear all 7 pieces off the board wins!
